A Placement Prediction system is a digital tool aimed at helping students and job seekers assess their likelihood of securing employment or internships. It utilizes various data points and advanced algorithms to provide users with valuable insights into their chances of success in the job market. By analyzing key factors, it offers a predictive approach to career opportunities, making the job search more informed and strategic.
